Rustwagon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Rustwagon is a single-player offline game. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (volume, sensitivity, brightness, touch button layout) and game progress are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the game.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions or support: oonursir@gmail.com
